What Is AEO?

Your customers are already asking ChatGPT and Perplexity questions like "best packaging companies in Alabama" and "custom box printing near me." Right now, GoldLeaf isn't showing up in those answers — but your competitors could be.

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is how you fix that. AI search engines don't show 10 blue links. They read your site, decide whether your content is trustworthy, and either cite you in their answers or ignore you completely. This playbook shows you exactly how to get cited.

Beyond the Scanner

1. Add llms.txt File

Missing (404)

99% of your competitors don't have this yet. It's a plain text file that tells AI systems exactly what GoldLeaf does — think of it as a "README for robots." Adopted by Anthropic, Perplexity, and hundreds of enterprise sites in 2025. 30 minutes to create, massive citation boost.

2. Add Schema Markup (JSON-LD) — Site-Wide

0/20 on all 55 pages

This is the single biggest problem. Zero structured data on any of the 55 pages scanned. Structured data is the language AI engines speak — without it, they can't understand what GoldLeaf does or when to cite you. This alone is worth +20 points per page.

The good news: your site footer already has the right contact data in crawlable HTML (Birmingham, AL address, phone, email on every page). The schema just needs to formalize what's already there so AI engines can parse it reliably. No visual changes required — schema lives in the source code, invisible to visitors. Zero design impact.

Required schema types:

Schema TypeWherePurpose
OrganizationEvery page (global template)Business identity, logo, contact
LocalBusinessHomepageLocation, hours, service area
FAQPage/faq + pages with Q&AQuestion-answer citation
ServiceEach service pageService descriptions
BlogPostingEach blog postArticle metadata, author, date
BreadcrumbListAll pagesSite navigation structure

Example — Organization (goes on every page):

{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Organization",
  "name": "GoldLeaf Print & Packaging",
  "url": "https://www.goldleafpackaging.com",
  "logo": "https://www.goldleafpackaging.com/logo.png",
  "description": "US-based premium custom printing company...",
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "addressLocality": "Birmingham",
    "addressRegion": "AL",
    "addressCountry": "US"
  }
}
4-6 hours effort +20 pts per page

6. Add Open Graph and Twitter Card Meta Tags

Not detected on any page

AI systems that surface web results (Perplexity, Bing Copilot) use OG data for rich previews. No OG tags = plain text links that get less attention.

<meta property="og:title" content="Custom Box Printing | GoldLeaf">
<meta property="og:description" content="Custom designed boxes...">
<meta property="og:image" content=".../custom-boxes-og.jpg">
<meta property="og:url" content=".../services/custom-boxes">
<meta name="twitter:card" content="summary_large_image">
1-2 hours effort Medium impact

7. Add Canonical URLs

Not detected

The site redirects goldleafpackaging.com → www.goldleafpackaging.com. Without canonical URLs, AI engines may split citation authority between duplicates.

<link rel="canonical" href="https://www.goldleafpackaging.com/services/custom-boxes">
30 min effort Medium impact

Tier 3 E-E-A-T Signals — Authority Building

Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness — the signals AI uses to decide if GoldLeaf is credible. Without these, you may show up in AI search but not get cited. These signals separate GoldLeaf from anonymous blogs and offshore printers.

9. Add Author Attribution to Blog Posts

No author names on any content

Anonymous blog posts score lower than attributed ones. Add bylines ("By [Name], [Title] at GoldLeaf"), Person schema, and LinkedIn links.

1-2 hours effort Medium impact

10. Add Publication and Update Dates

No visible dates on any content

AI systems prioritize fresh content. Blog posts with no date are treated as potentially outdated. Add publication dates visibly and in datePublished / dateModified schema.

1 hour effort Medium impact

11. Surface Awards and Certifications

"Award-winning" is mentioned but no specific awards are displayed. The /about page mentions "50 years of collective experience" and "3.2k Clients / 4.3m Items" — great proof points that should be structured and highlighted.

1 hour effort Medium impact

Tier 4 Content Strategy — Ongoing

Long-term content investments that compound over time. Once the technical foundation is in place (Tiers 1–3), this is how GoldLeaf becomes the authority that AI engines cite for packaging topics in Alabama and beyond.

13. Write "Answer-First" Content

AI engines extract direct answers. Content that leads with the answer gets cited more.

Current style: "In the world of packaging, there are many factors to consider..."

AEO-optimized: "The best packaging materials for food products are: kraft paper, corrugated cardboard, and food-grade polyethylene. Here's when to use each..."

14. Create "Best Of" and Comparison Content

Target the exact queries people ask AI assistants:

  • "Best Packaging Materials for [Industry]" (cannabis, food, cosmetics)
  • "Digital vs. Offset Printing: Which Is Right for Your Packaging?"
  • "Custom Box Types: A Complete Guide"
  • "Label Materials Compared: Paper vs. Film vs. Synthetic"

15. Add a Glossary or Knowledge Base

A /glossary page defining printing terms (GSM, dieline, substrate, CMYK, spot UV, etc.) creates dozens of citable definitions. AI systems frequently cite glossary entries.
